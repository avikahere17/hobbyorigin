## 1. Executive Summary

HobbyOrigin is a community discovery and engagement platform connecting people of all ages around shared hobbies, interests, and local activities. Unlike generic social networks, HobbyOrigin is:

- **Age-inclusive** — purpose-built for kids, teens, adults, and seniors with adaptive interfaces
- **Hyper-local** — connecting people at building, neighbourhood, and city level
- **Monetisation-rich** — seven distinct revenue streams embedded natively in the product
- **Community-safe** — parental controls, age-gated groups, and community moderation

We are seeking **£1.5 million in seed investment** to fund 18 months of product development, team scaling, and UK market launch, targeting **50,000 MAU and £500k GMV in Year 1**.

## 4. Revenue Model

HobbyOrigin operates a **multi-sided marketplace model** with seven revenue streams, designed for compounding growth as the community scales.

---

### 4.1 Kids' Contribution Wallet (Parental Monetisation)

**How it works:**  
Parents create a linked parent account and allocate a digital contribution wallet to each child. Children earn virtual coins by actively participating in groups (attending sessions, completing activities, positive peer interactions). Parents top up wallets via Stripe.

**Revenue mechanisms:**
- Platform takes **5% on wallet top-ups** (parents are the payer, low friction)
- Parents can tip tutors directly through the platform — **10% platform fee on tutor tips**
- Coins can be redeemed for premium features (stickers, profile customisation) — **direct revenue**
- Charity donation option (1% of donation value returned as platform credit)

**Year 1 estimate:** 8,000 parent accounts × £5/month average wallet spend × 5% = **£24,000/year**

---

### 4.2 Tutor Tips & Awards (Tutor Monetisation)

**How it works:**  
Specialist tutors create groups with scheduled sessions. After each session, attendees (or their parents) can send tips or award badges. The platform facilitates payment via Stripe Connect.

**Revenue mechanisms:**
- **10% platform commission** on all tips and session fees collected through the platform
- Tutor **subscription plan** — £9.99/month for premium tutor profile (verified badge, analytics, priority listing)
- Featured tutor placement in group discovery — **£19.99/month per tutor**

**Unit economics:**
- Average tutor earns £120/month in tips
- 500 tutors × £120 × 10% = **£6,000/month = £72,000/year**
- Premium subscriptions: 200 tutors × £9.99 = **£24,000/year**
- **Total stream estimate: £96,000/year**

---

### 4.3 Club Membership Subscriptions

**How it works:**  
Community clubs (residential buildings, local associations, sports clubs) can offer paid membership via HobbyOrigin. Members get exclusive access to private groups, discounted services, and early event booking.

**Revenue mechanisms:**
- Platform takes **8% of subscription revenue** from clubs
- Club starter pack (onboarding, setup, first month free): **£99 setup fee**
- Enterprise club plan (100+ members, custom branding, analytics): **£99/month**

**Year 1 estimate:**
- 300 clubs, average 40 paying members at £5/month
- 300 × 40 × £5 × 8% = **£57,600/year**
- 50 enterprise clubs × £99/month = **£59,400/year**
- **Total stream estimate: £117,000/year**

---

### 4.4 Marketing Campaigns for Special Groups

**How it works:**  
Group organisers and clubs create targeted marketing campaigns to grow membership, promote events, or raise awareness. Campaign tools include social card generation, email broadcasts (via SendGrid), in-app push, and audience targeting by age group, city, and interests.

**Revenue mechanisms:**
- **Self-serve campaign credits** — £10 per 1,000 in-app impressions (CPM model)
- **Managed campaign packages** — £299 (Basic: 2-week campaign, 10k impressions), £799 (Pro: 4-week, 50k impressions, email blast)
- **Sponsored group placement** in group discovery feed — £49/week per group

**Year 1 estimate:**
- 500 campaigns/year at average £150 blended spend
- **£75,000/year**

---

### 4.5 Club Product Sales & Commerce

**How it works:**  
Clubs can open a Club Shop within their group page to sell physical products (pottery kits, art supplies, craft packs) or digital products (worksheets, guides, patterns). Stripe handles checkout; fulfilment is the club's responsibility.

**Revenue mechanisms:**
- **12% commission** on all product sales through the platform
- **Digital product listing fee** — £2.99 per digital product listed
- Premium shop features (custom banner, featured products, discount codes): **£14.99/month**

**Year 1 estimate:**
- £250,000 GMV in product sales × 12% = **£30,000/year**
- Digital listings and premium shops: **£18,000/year**
- **Total stream estimate: £48,000/year**

---

### 4.6 Virtual Hosted Events with Marketing Campaigns

**How it works:**  
Clubs, tutors, and specialists host virtual events (live video sessions) on the platform. Events have a registration page, capacity, description, and an optional paid ticket. Organisers can launch a marketing campaign alongside the event to drive registrations.

**Revenue mechanisms:**
- **Ticket sales commission** — 8% on paid virtual event tickets
- **Event marketing bundle** — £149 (event listing + social card + email to city followers)
- **Premium virtual event features** — recording, Q&A moderation, branded waiting room: **£49/event**
- **Sponsored event placement** in homepage discovery: **£79/event slot**

**Year 1 estimate:**
- 2,000 events/year; 30% paid tickets at average £8/ticket, average 20 attendees
- 600 paid events × 20 × £8 × 8% = **£7,680**
- Marketing bundles and premium features: **£80,000/year**
- **Total stream estimate: £88,000/year**

---

### 4.7 Mentorship & Volunteer Specialist Services

**How it works:**  
Verified specialist volunteers (retired professionals, academics, industry experts) can offer structured mentorship sessions to groups. Organisations (e.g. charities, councils) can sponsor mentorship access for underserved communities (kids, seniors).

**Revenue mechanisms:**
- **Institutional sponsor packages** — councils / charities pay £500–£2,000/month to fund free access for their communities
- **Verified specialist badge** — £29/year for professionals who wish to be listed as specialists
- **Mentorship marketplace listing fee** — £4.99/month per specialist offering sessions

**Year 1 estimate:**
- 20 institutional sponsors × £800/month average = **£192,000/year**
- Specialist badges and listings: **£30,000/year**
- **Total stream estimate: £222,000/year**

## 5. Go-to-Market Strategy

### 5.1 Phase 1 — Anchor Communities (Q3–Q4 2026)
- Partner with **5 residential building management companies** to onboard Elm Court-style communities
- Partner with **3 UK councils** (social isolation programmes) for subsidised senior access
- Soft launch in **London, Manchester, Edinburgh**
- Recruit 100 founding tutors via direct outreach + hobbyist forums

### 5.2 Phase 2 — Organic + Paid Growth (Q1 2027)
- Referral programme: existing users get premium credits for inviting neighbours
- SEO + content: "find hobby groups near me" long-tail strategy
- Influencer partnerships with parenting and crafting communities on Instagram/TikTok
- Press: education, community, and senior-living publications

### 5.3 Phase 3 — Platform Expansion (Q2–Q3 2027)
- Launch native iOS and Android apps
- B2B: sell Club Suite to community leisure centres, housing associations, and libraries
- International: Ireland → Canada → Australia (English-speaking diaspora communities)
