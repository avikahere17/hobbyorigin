Usage Guide

🌐 Website header

Use logo-horizontal.svg at 180–220px wide. Always on dark background (#0d0d1a or #13131f).

📱 Mobile app

Use logo-icon.svg at 48×48px for navigation, 120×120px for splash/onboarding screens.

💼 LinkedIn company page

Logo: logo-icon.svg exported at 300×300px PNG. Banner: use gradient dark background with wordmark.

🖨️ Print / Documents

Use logo-white-bg.svg for light backgrounds (PDFs, pitch decks, business cards).

🐦 Social media avatar

Export logo-icon.svg as 400×400px PNG. Works for LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, WhatsApp.

📊 Pitch deck

Use logo-primary.svg on the title slide. Use logo-horizontal.svg in headers of subsequent slides.
