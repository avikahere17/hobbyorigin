👤 FOUNDER STORY

I had no idea how to code. Here's how I built a full-stack app anyway.

My name is Avika Garg. I live in Thubrahalli, Bangalore.

I watched kids in my society play alone when there were other kids with the same interests one floor up. I watched seniors become invisible as life moved online.

I had no technical background. No coding degree. No engineering co-founder.

What I had: a very clear problem. And an AI I could describe it to precisely.

In 90 days, using Claude (Anthropic AI), I built:

→ React web app + React Native mobile
→ GraphQL API with real-time chat
→ Virtual events, club shop, tutor tips, parental controls
→ Support for UK 🇬🇧, USA 🇺🇸, India 🇮🇳

"The barrier to building isn't technical skill. It's clarity of purpose."

About

HobbyOrigin is a hyper-local community platform connecting people of all ages around shared hobbies — in their own building, street, and city.

We believe loneliness is a design problem. Our platform features age-adaptive design, hyper-local discovery, real-time group chat, virtual events, tutor tipping, club shops, campaign management, and parental controls.

Available in the UK 🇬🇧, USA 🇺🇸, and India 🇮🇳. Supporting GBP, USD, and INR. Built in Bangalore by Avika Garg.
