Our story

Built by someone who lived the problem

Avika Garg is the founder and CEO of HobbyOrigin. Based in E704 UKN Esperanza, Thubrahalli, Bangalore, she grew up watching kids in her housing society go unnoticed — bright, creative children with nowhere to channel their energy and no one nearby who shared their interests. She watched her elderly neighbours grow invisible as the world moved online.

Avika had no technical background. No coding degree. No VC network. What she had was clarity about the problem and the courage to describe it precisely — to an AI, one conversation at a time.

In 90 days, using Claude (Anthropic AI) as her engineering team, she built a full-stack platform: React web app, React Native mobile app, GraphQL API, real-time chat, virtual events, a club shop, campaign tools, parental controls, a tutor tipping system, a global expert network with charity & paid sessions, and a dedicated elder care support system — all from her flat in Bangalore.

🌟
Core belief:

Everyone deserves access to expertise — not just the privileged few. Technology should reduce loneliness, not cause it. And no senior should grow old without community. The barrier to building isn't technical skill — it's clarity of purpose.
